Layout-structure observations (from product photos/examples, all Observed / Estimated) #

  • Monthly calendar (coil/disc planners): Title zone on top (month + year), weekday header row (S M T W T F S), 7-column grid, 6 rows on commercial monthlies (covers all month shapes), date numeral top-left of cell, optional right or bottom sidebar for notes/goals. Erin Condren monthly includes a notes sidebar; Happy Planner monthly is full-bleed grid with a sidebar column.
  • Weekly vertical (EC/Happy Planner): 7 day-columns; each column split into 3 stacked sections (morning / afternoon / evening); Happy Planner Classic vertical also ships a "dashboard" variant with priorities + to-do sidebar.
  • Weekly horizontal: 7 stacked horizontal day-rows across the page/spread; each row: day label at left + lined writing area.
  • Hourly weekly: left time column (hours 6 AM–9 PM typical; Passion Planner 6 AM–11 PM in 30-min rows), 7 day columns.
  • Daily planner: date/title header, hourly timeline (15–17 rows), top-3 priorities box, to-do list, notes section.
  • Hobonichi Cousin weekly: 2-page spread, 24-hour vertical timetable per day.
  • Desk pads monthly: header/ad-copy band on top, 7-column × 5–6-row grid, Julian dates + reference calendars common.

1.4 Recommended Product Studio Defaults — Planners #

(Studio defaults — separated from manufacturer data; prioritize handwriting space, print safety, easy generation.)

  • Page margins: coil/disc punched edge 0.75"–1.0"; outer/top/bottom 0.5"; book gutter per KDP table (0.375–0.625 by page count).
  • Cell/box padding: 0.08"–0.12" (2–3 mm).
  • Monthly: title 0.7"–0.8"; weekday header 0.35"; 6 rows always; date numeral 12 pt top-left; sidebar 1.4" when used.
  • Weekly vertical: 7 columns; 3 sections/day; section labels 9 pt small caps.
  • Hourly: time column 0.8"; hourly rows 0.35"–0.6" by page height; default hours 6 AM–9 PM; half-hour = half of hourly.
  • Daily: date header 0.6"; hourly rows 0.3"–0.35"; priorities box ≥1.0"; notes ≥1.2".
  • Line spacing (lined areas): 0.28"–0.35" (7–9 mm).
  • Typography: month title 20–24 pt; weekday 9–10 pt uppercase +letterspace; date numerals 12 pt; section heads 10–11 pt bold; body 9–10 pt; tiny labels ≥6.5 pt.
  • Grid rules: 0.5–0.75 pt; header rules 1–1.5 pt.
  • Disc-bound writing-safe margin: nothing functional within 0.5" of punched edge (holes ~0.98" apart).
  • Desk pads: monthly block ≥2.25" × 2.25" on 22×17; weekly pad rows ≥1.75" tall; top glue zone 0.75".

2.4 Recommended Product Studio Defaults — Block A #
